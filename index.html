How to calculate craft fair break-even

Subtract product costs and average payment fees from revenue per order. Divide your fixed event costs by the amount left per order, then round up. If orders contribute zero or less, selling more at the same price cannot cover positive fixed costs.

In the sample, each order contributes $14.98 toward $105 in fixed costs. Seven orders leave you $0.14 short, and eight orders cover the booth. Try a second calculation with a slower sales day before booking.
